What the project does

The problem

A fixed signal plan gives every direction the same green time all day. When one road is busy and the cross road is empty, vehicles queue while green time goes to waste.

The approach

A small neural network reads smoothed detector counts for the four approaches each cycle and recommends the split between the North–South and East–West phases.

The evidence

A second-by-second simulation of a full day compares the AI plan against a fixed 40 s / 40 s plan over five random days.
